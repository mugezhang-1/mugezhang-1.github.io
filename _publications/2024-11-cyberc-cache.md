IoT devices have little local storage and depend on distant cloud servers, so latency is dominated by what has to be fetched remotely. This paper frames the device as a cache and the cloud as memory, and uses lightweight AI methods to predict users' behavior patterns so that the data most likely to be needed next is already on the device.
